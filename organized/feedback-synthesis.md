# Customer Feedback Synthesis
*Patterns across January - April (178 total survey responses)*

## The Big Picture

The loyalty program is **"fine but forgettable"** - a phrase that appeared independently in three separate months. People don't hate it. They just don't care. And that might be worse.

Meanwhile, Basecamp Coffee itself is loved (NPS: 67). The rewards program is not (NPS: 12). There's a massive gap between how people feel about the brand and how they feel about the program.

## Key Themes

### 1. Invisible Program
- Customers forget the app is on their phone
- Average app session: 23 seconds (just checking points)
- Multiple people said they "forgot I was a member"
- Baristas are the only reason people scan at all

### 2. Zero Personality
- "Generic. Could be any coffee chain."
- "This program has no personality. It's just points."
- "Doesn't feel like Basecamp. Feels like corporate stuff."
- The flyer literally says "the Coffee Shop" - even the marketing materials are generic

### 3. Tiers Nobody Understands
- Trailblazer, Explorer, Summit - nobody knows what they mean
- Customers can't explain the benefits of moving up
- Baristas can't explain it either
- "Is there any reason to hit the higher tiers?"

### 4. Transactional, Not Personal
- "I don't feel like a valued customer, I feel like a transaction"
- "The app treats me like a stranger" (from a 2-year regular)
- People want to be *known*, not just *counted*

### 5. Competitors Are Doing It Better
- Starbucks gets mentioned for gamification and rewards
- Dutch Bros gets mentioned for personal touch and remembering names
- Basecamp is doing neither - stuck in the middle

## Satisfaction Trend (Declining)

| Month | Very Satisfied + Satisfied | Neutral | Dissatisfied |
|-------|---------------------------|---------|-------------|
| Jan   | 46%                       | 41%     | 13%         |
| Feb   | 41%                       | 44%     | 15%         |
| Mar   | 37%                       | 47%     | 16%         |
| Apr   | 34%                       | 48%     | 18%         |

Satisfaction is sliding month over month. Neutral is growing - people are checking out.

## Hidden Signals Worth Noting

- "I wish you could help me find new drinks to try"
- "What if you helped people find their signature drink?"
- "People light up when we ask about their coffee preferences"
- "Your coffee has personality. Your rewards program doesn't. Fix that."

Customers are practically *telling us* what they want - something personal, something that feels like Basecamp, something about the coffee itself.

## Action Items

1. **Stop** investing in tier name changes or minor point adjustments - the problem is deeper
2. **Investigate** what "personality" means for a rewards program
3. **Explore** personalization - customers want to be known
4. **Bridge the gap** between the Basecamp brand people love and the generic program they ignore
5. **Talk to baristas** - they're the closest to customers and already doing the personal touch the app fails to deliver
