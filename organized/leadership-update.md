## Loyalty Program - Customer Feedback Review
**Date:** February 16, 2026
**From:** Basecamp Coffee Customer Experience Team

### Status
At Risk

### Summary
Our loyalty program is not resonating with customers. Across 178 survey responses (Jan-Apr), satisfaction has dropped from 46% to 34%, and the program NPS (12) lags far behind our brand NPS (67). Customers describe the program as "fine but forgettable" - they love Basecamp Coffee but are disengaged from the rewards experience.

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Current | Target | Trend |
|--------|---------|--------|-------|
| Program NPS | 12 | 67 (brand parity) | Down |
| Satisfaction (Very Satisfied + Satisfied) | 34% (Apr) | 50%+ | Down |
| Neutral/Disengaged | 48% (Apr) | <30% | Up |
| Avg App Session | 23 seconds | - | Flat |

### Progress Since Last Update
- Completed full feedback synthesis across 4 months of survey data (178 responses)
- Identified 5 core themes driving disengagement: invisibility, lack of personality, confusing tiers, transactional feel, and competitive gap
- Surfaced strong customer signals pointing toward personalization and coffee discovery as opportunities

### Blockers / Risks
- Satisfaction is declining month-over-month with no sign of stabilizing
- Competitors (Starbucks, Dutch Bros) are outperforming us on both gamification and personal connection
- Baristas are currently the only thing keeping customers engaged with the program - the app and tiers are not pulling their weight
- Incremental fixes (tier renaming, point adjustments) will not address the root issue

### Next Steps
- Investigate what a "personality-driven" rewards experience looks like for Basecamp
- Explore personalization features (e.g., drink discovery, preference tracking)
- Conduct barista interviews to capture what's already working at the human level
- Develop a proposal for a program refresh aligned with the Basecamp brand

### Decision Needed (if any)
We need leadership alignment on whether to pursue a full loyalty program redesign vs. incremental improvements. The data strongly suggests incremental changes won't close the gap. Recommend approving a discovery sprint to scope a redesign.
